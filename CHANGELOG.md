# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Stone Tiles, with stairs, slab and wall
- Diorite Bricks, Mossy Diorite Bricks and Diorite Tiles, each with stairs, slab and wall
- Cracked Diorite Bricks and Chiseled Diorite
- Stonecutter recipes for every block and recipe book unlocks for every recipe
- A Cohesion creative tab, plus placement next to the vanilla stone bricks and diorite in the Building Blocks tab

### Changed

- Every stairs crafting recipe, vanilla and Cohesion, now takes 3 blocks (top left, middle left, middle) for 4 stairs
- Iron and copper chains are crafted 8 at a time instead of 1
- All trapdoors are crafted 6 at a time instead of 2 (iron and copper gave 1)
- All doors are crafted 2 at a time instead of 3

### Development

- Models, names, recipes, recipe unlocks, loot tables and tags are generated with Fabric datagen
- A resource checker and a datagen up-to-date check run on every push
